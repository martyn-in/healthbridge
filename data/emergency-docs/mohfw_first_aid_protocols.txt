GOVERNMENT OF INDIA - MINISTRY OF HEALTH AND FAMILY WELFARE (MoHFW) EMERGENCY FIRST AID PROTOCOLS

DOCUMENT SUMMARY:
National emergency first-aid protocols issued by MoHFW / National Health Portal of India for stroke, acute myocardial infarction (heart attack), severe trauma, heatstroke, and unconsciousness.

1. ACUTE STROKE RECOGNITION AND FIRST-AID (FAST PROTOCOL):
- Immediate Actions:
  1. Check FAST signs immediately:
     - Face: Ask the person to smile. Does one side of the face droop?
     - Arms: Ask the person to raise both arms. Does one arm drift downward?
     - Speech: Ask the person to repeat a simple sentence. Is speech slurred or strange?
     - Time: If any of these signs are present, call 112 / 108 emergency services immediately.
  2. Keep the person lying flat on their side (recovery position) with head slightly elevated if conscious.
  3. Note the exact time symptoms started (critical for thrombolytic clot-busting treatment window).
- What to Avoid:
  1. Do NOT give food, water, aspirin, or oral medications before medical evaluation.
  2. Do NOT allow the patient to drive or sleep off symptoms.
- Warning Signs:
  Sudden numbness or weakness in face, arm, or leg (especially on one side), sudden confusion, trouble seeing in one or both eyes, sudden severe headache with no known cause.

2. ACUTE CARDIAC EVENT / SUSPECTED HEART ATTACK:
- Immediate Actions:
  1. Have the person stop all physical activity immediately and sit down in a comfortable position (semi-recumbent, back supported).
  2. Call Emergency Ambulance (112 / 108) immediately.
  3. Loosen tight clothing around the neck, chest, and waist.
  4. If trained and patient has prescribed sublingual nitroglycerin or chewable adult aspirin (300mg), assist them if advised by emergency physician, provided no contraindications exist.
  5. If patient collapses and stops breathing normally, begin immediate CPR (30 chest compressions to 2 breaths).
- What to Avoid:
  1. Do NOT leave the patient unattended.
  2. Do NOT allow patient to walk or exert physical effort.
- Warning Signs:
  Crushing chest pain or tightness radiating to left arm, neck, jaw, or back, shortness of breath, cold sweats, dizziness, nausea.

3. SEVERE HEATSTROKE AND HYPERTHERMIA:
- Immediate Actions:
  1. Move the victim immediately to a cool, shaded, or air-conditioned area.
  2. Remove outer heavy clothing.
  3. Cool the body rapidly: spray or sponge with cool water, fan vigorously, and place ice packs or cold wet towels on neck, armpits, and groin.
  4. Call 112 / 108 emergency transport.
- What to Avoid:
  1. Do NOT give fluids if victim is confused, drowsy, or vomiting.
  2. Do NOT use alcohol rubs.
- Warning Signs:
  High body temperature (>104°F / 40°C), hot red dry or damp skin, altered mental status, delirium, loss of consciousness, seizures.
